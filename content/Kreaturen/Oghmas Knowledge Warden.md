_Medium Construct (abberation), lawful neutral_

**Armor Class** 18 (otherworldly alloy)  
**Hit Points** >150
**Speed** fly 40 ft. (hover)

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 22 (+6) | 26 (+8) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Int +12, Wis +7, Cha +8
**Damage Resistances** radiant, psychic, bps from nonmagical, non-adamantine weapons  
**Damage Immunities** poison
**Condition Immunities** poisoned, exhausted, charmed, frightened, paralyzed, disease 
**Senses** truesight 60 ft., passive Perception 17
**Languages** all languages, telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP), PB +4

---

Special Traits
 
- **Hover** - Ignores difficult terrain and moves silently over the ground.
- **Isolation Lock** - Locks down the current area so that the only way to leave is to defeat the warden or by passing a DC 25 Con save.
- **Database** - Keeps a database of all attacks that have hit it. When it gets hit by an attack, it automatically gets Tier 1. This database persists between loops.
	Tier 1 - Attack was registered.
	Tier 2 - The attack has advantage against the warden but it has advantage on _Refine Simulation_.
	Tier 3 - Immediately after the attack hits the warden, it can taunt the attacker (DC 16 Wis save) to repeat the attack next time it would use an attack if possible. It cannot taunt on an attack that was taunted.
	Tier 4 - The attacker heals the amount of damage he deals to the warden.
	Tier 5 - The warden is immune to critical hits from the attack.
	Tier 6 - The warden only takes half of the damage from the attack.
	Tier 7 - The warden is immune against the attack.

---

ACTIONS

**Multiattack.** Attack half as many times as other attacks hit you since the end of your last turn.

**Replay attack.** Use one of the attacks stored in the database.
	Tier 1 - Do not add your proficiency bonus.
	Tier 2 - Attack with Disadvantage
	Tier 3 - Attack normally
	Tier 4 - Attack with advantage
	Tier 5 - Automatically hit
	Tier 6 - Always crit
	Tier 7 - Player crit
   

BONUS ACTION

**Reverse Engineering.** Rewind your memory audibly and visibly and produce effects you have recorded recently. Use any ability, spell, etc. that has been used since the end of your last turn

---

### **Legendary Actions** (3/round)

- **Refine Simulation** - When the warden gets hit by an attack, it can make an Int save (+12) equal to the attack roll. If it succeeds, increase the Tier of the data on the attack.
