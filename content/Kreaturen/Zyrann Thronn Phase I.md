SPECIAL TRAITS

- **Static Position.** The Zyrann Thronn cannot move and thus fails all Dexterity saving throws. It cannot be moved or teleported due to the connection with its Lair.
- **Random Access Turns.** Players may take their turns in any order. The ZT will take its turn last.

### Legendary Actions & Reactions
Can be done at the end of a creatures turn in any act if not specified.
- **Conduct Attack.** *End of enemy creatures turn.* The closest three Instruments to the creature that just took its turn, take their turn.
- **Adaptive Blood.** *End of ZTs turn.* The ZT can transmute its rainbow blood and remove one effect from itself that prevents it from acting.
- **Calculating Upwards Trajectory.** *End of enemy creatures turn.* Move the creature that just took its turn up by 5ft. for every damage instance the ZT received that turn.
- **Imaginary Transposition. (Act 2)** *End of ZTs turn.* The enemy creature that dealt the most damage this round must succeed on a DC 23 Cha save or get banished to the ZTs demiplane until the end of their next turn. If they succeed, the enemy with the next highest damage will be targeted until all options are exhausted or one fails the save. (See below for Banishment Condition)
- **Invest Potionblood. (Act 3)** *End of enemy creatures turn.* The ZT summons an Elemental, which then takes its turn. It has 1 hp, one attack (& is immune to the elemental effects)
	- Lightning Elemental - Shoots itself as a damaging projectile to the next lightning zone (triggers opp att)
	- Stone Elemental - Does not move, has reach, and if you enter its reach or start your turn there it makes an opp att, if it hits targets movement is 0
	- Ice Elemental - Leaves a trail where you lose half movement if you enter it.
	- Lava Elemental - Long range glass projectiles, hellish rebuke 1d6

### Lair Action
- **Local Area Broadcast. (Act 1)** *End of the round.* All grounded enemies of the ZT in its lair receive damage equal to the amount of Instruments still alive. Targets may do a DC 14 Dex save and take half as much damage on a success.
- **Open Dimensional Rift. (Act 2)**. *Start of the act.* Turn on the fountains sprouting water from the demiplane out of the walls. Entering a fountain moves a creature to its end.
	- *End of the round.* The fountains fill up the arena (R1 - Moat, R2 - 0ft, R3 - 5ft, act 3 full arena).
- **Forging Connections. (Act 2)** *Start of the act.* The ZT equips all pillars with metal contraptions created in the four forges. These contraptions draw on the Rainbowfluid the pillars provide to grow crystals that open dimensional rifts to the 4 elemental planes. A creature next to the pillar can decide to drain the pillar of its supply of Rainbowfluid or take on an elemental aspect by letting the contraption drain it. ==This aspect can be used until the end of the next round. ==
	- Aspect of Wind - All creatures in a 15ft. aura get pushed away from you when they enter or start their turn there unless they succeed on a DC 23 Dex save.
	- Aspect of Fire - A fiery aura applies the Burning condition to creatures and flammable objects in a 0ft. radius. You can increase the auras radius up to your base movement speed. ==For every 5ft. you will be moved 5ft. in a direction of your choice.==
	- Aspect of Water - You gain a swim speed equal to your highest speed and waterbreathing.
	- Aspect of Earth - You cannot be moved by force.
- **Manifesting Tunnel. (Act 3)** *Start of the act.* On each pillar a hazard zone is created based on the connected elemental plane.
	- Lightning Zone - When exiting the Lightning Zone a creature will be forcefully moved to a position chosen by the ZT. The affected creature can use an action to choose the position itself.
	- Lava Zone - When moving inside the Lava Zone movement costs 1 health instead of 5 feet per square, if the creature is not immune to forced movement.
	- Ice Zone - When entering the Ice Zone or starting its turn there, a creatures AC and Dex Saves are reduced by 2 until the start of its next turn.
	- Quicksand Zone - When entering the Quicksand Zone or starting its turn there, a creature will be moved to a random position inside the zone.
- **Computation complete. (Act 4)**** *End of the act.* The ZT opens the now prepared ceiling up to ascend into and out of the lake, to complete its evolution and summon its new body with a huge psychokinetic explosion. This will result in a shockwave going through the Plateau which will deal damage equal to the remaining HP of the ZT to all structures connected to the ground of the plateau.
