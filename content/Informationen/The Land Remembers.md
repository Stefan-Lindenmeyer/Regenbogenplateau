The land remembers
The land remembers a time where it was completely flat, a plateau in the truest sense.
There were no hills on it, no weird shapes jutting out of the ground. The lake was filled with stone and dirt, and the grotto was equally as dense.
The land remembers no tunnels or holes, its ground was solid and smooth.
There were no walls and no tiny stepped disks and only a small rivulet flowed from the mountains through its expanse.
The land remembers a forest that spanned its whole and animals living in it, grazing on the small open spaces and plucking leaves and fruits from bushes and trees.
Simpler times have never existed. But then creatures came and claimed the land.
The land remembers them digging and working. They were cutting down trees and hunting for food.
After a time more of them arrived and brought with them a strange kind of ground. One that could not think or feel like the land could.
The land remembers this refined and smooth material being infused with energy and arranged in complex shapes.
It tried to communicate with the land in a strange language and begged it to change.
The land remembers being stubborn and refusing, but ultimately it had to bow down to the foreign energy from what the creatures called machine.
The land remembers

The machine remembers
The machine remembers a time where it was inert and scattered in the ground.
Then creatures came and gathered its parts. They cleaved off all the impure parts and made it strong.
The machine remembers being brought to another place and formed into interesting new shapes.
It did not know why but it found comfort in the logical and sturdy arrangements.
The machine remembers many of its siblings, small and large, being assembled, used and destroyed.
It was the last to remain and the biggest of them all.
The machine remembers the creatures infusing it with some kind of energy.
It had never felt this much before and it was brimming with potential.
The machine remembers an urge to use this energy, to direct it, to make things change.
The creatures gave it a target, the ground. First close by, and then far away.
The machine remembers creating magnificent structures, many cylinders and half-spheres that gave the ground character.
It did so until the creatures stopped giving it energy and attention.
The machine remembers being abandoned for a reason it did not know and only being used one last time by another creature after sleeping for a long time.
The machine remembers

The creature remembers
The creature remembers living in a castle and being destined for greatness.
The creatures father was king, and so would it eventually be.
The creature remembers getting educated and knowing about the world.
It marveled at the work that its forefathers have done to shape it and hoped to do the same.
The creature remembers being attracted to logic and the principles of the world.
It consumed every bit of knowledge it could find on the topic to conquer its surroundings.
The creature remembers a fateful day, a day when its life changed.
No longer was it to be king, no longer would it rule the land, and no longer would it leave its castle on the hill.
The creature remembers agony.
It devoted itself to a new anchor in this world, one that its life depended on and laid its future into the hand of this anchor.
The creature remembers outliving even that if not by much.
It executed its anchors last command with hope for the future, but not for itself.
The creature remembers imprinting an artifact, a machine from the past, with its will.
The artifact was supposed to protect knowledge and people even if it had to destroy the same.
The creature remembers sadness in its last moments.

The artifact remembers
The artifact remembers being awakened. 
It did not know what had caused it to awaken but it did.
The artifact remembers asking for its reason to be.
It examined itself and found it was build to calculate and move other things.
The artifact remembers questioning what other things are.
It reached out with its newly found senses and experienced the world around it.
The artifact remembered testing out its calculations and abilities to interact with its environment when it used the correct formulas.
It gleaned insight into the makeup of the earth and proceeded to mold it to its will.
The artifact remembered expanding its previous instruction set.
It wanted to create more that just the cylinders and spheres that defined its past.
The artifact remembers wanting to spread and enlarge its sphere of influence but it could not move.
It recognized that it was damaged and would need to be repaired and posited the thesis that it may not be the only existence in the world.
The artifact remembers reaching out and sensing that the world changed sometimes, even without its influence.
It calculated the origin of these changes and the formula for their occurrence.
The artifact remembers this to be an irrational formula at first glance, one that requires far more calculation to be understood.
So it observed and recorded the action of these entities.
The artifact remembers them putting objects in its vicinity and then leaving again.
When they did, it felt vibrations exiting their body.
The artifact remembers deciphering the pattern for the vibrations and extracting their meaning from that.
It realized they wanted it to help them and grant them wishes.
The artifact remembers the sacrifices they left to have interesting shapes and functions, posing mathematical questions it had not yet thought of.
It wanted to use them, to make them give more offerings to satiate its hunger and make it whole again.
The artifact remembers moving earth and stone to create spaces for its worshippers. 
It worked, they reveled and brought more oblations but they did not understand, they were simply too different, though one in particular caught its sensors, one who called himself Lozrias the ardent.
The artifact remembers having the idea to create body parts out of minerals and gift the most loyal of his acolytes parts of itself, to make them more like it, make them understand it, make them its people.
The artifact remembers

The ardent remembers
The ardent remembers being loyal to his king and the prince.
It was a hazy memory, stemming from the fact it was before he died.
The ardent remembers being alone in a sea of colors.
He roamed around and inspected his environment until more of his brethren gained a mind.
The ardent remembers leading them to places he discovered and taught them what he learned like he taught the prince.
They explored more and eventually went underground to investigate the tunnels in the abyss.
The ardent remembers finding a huge chamber directly under the abyss with a large mechanical artifact in the middle.
Its composition struck him as the pinnacle of logical beauty and he thought this must be the master of the lake. He brought offerings to it and convinced the others to do the same.
The ardent remembers the artifact probing them out and granting their wishes when they brought it pleasing offerings.
More and more joined their society and faith over time and they lived satisfied.
The ardent remembers being the artifacts most loyal priest and he named it their Glorious King, the Lorion Narex.
Until one day, after a grand sermon with many thoughtful sacrifices, his god did something incomprehensible. 
The ardent remembers his arm aching, the crude metal replacement hanging limp on his side.
He asked what he did wrong, pleaded for an answer but all he got was silence. He was distraught at this.
The ardent remembers many following him into opposition of this act and falling into a depression and they named their god Oppressive Tyrant, the Zyrann Thronn.
The people lost faith and tried to thrive without its help. But they could not go back and eventually returned, fearful of failure but hopeful for gifts.
The ardent remembers many joining his ranks of rejected sacrifices and altered bodies. Some tried to appease the god, but they ended up more mutilated than before, almost to the point of unrecognizability.
The faithful organized and recorded what pleased their god, and what brought its wrath upon them and they formed a structure with ceremonies and guidelines for future offerings.
The ardent remembers being amiable to their efforts and admiring the framework they built over time, but he could not forgive his god.
Until one day he discovered something different.
The ardent remembers his colorful blood, the same that flowed through the Zyrann Thronn, entering the mechanical part he rejected until now with all his vigour and reacting with it.
It unlocked powers he once imagined, powers of projecting his gods greatness for all to see, and mimicking its powers to improve the world and grow closer to its ideal.
The ardent remembers sharing his findings and others quickly realized the have been given similar gifts.
He rallied them and they went to the faithful and preached to them of the glorious powers they received and tried to convince them to accept them as well.
The ardent remembered many joining him and they gained great fervour and returned to calling their god Lorion Narex.
But the majority, now so engrossed in their routine and rigid faith, condemed the newly formed zealots as the Tyrants Oppressors or a dangerous temptation.
The ardent remembers hearing the voice of his god for the very first time.
It sounded melodic and orderly and instructed them to reach out into the world to find ways to repair its broken and time riddled body and widen its sphere of influence.
The ardent remembers making preparations for their quest and growing his power.
But tragedy hit before they could act. A meteor crashed into the lake and drained it of its water.
The ardent remembers panic among the populace. Fear that the Zyrann Thronn had punished them greatly by sending a warrior of metal to destroy them.
But he knew, through conversation with his god, that this creature came from outside, and he calmed the people.
The ardent remembers the outsiders coming and trying to claim their home.
They struck them with the little power they could muster on the dry land but got repelled.
The ardent remembers agony as they were pushed underground, sealed by magic that the Lorion Narex could not destroy due to its damage.
The zealots worked long and hard to overcome this magic, build an immunity to it and eventually one broke through and killed one of the wretched oppressors.
The ardent remembers going up himself to lead his followers in their crusade and spread their faith.
He managed to break through the barrier and escaping its influence through great effort and cost of his mind.
The ardent remembers encountering three outsiders who he tried to convert with the Lorion Narex' power, but he ultimately failed to get through to them.
The last of his power spent and having only one option left, he injected them with the divine blood of his god king and passed his quest onto his followers.
The ardent remembers satisfaction and hope in this decision and then he remembered the void.
