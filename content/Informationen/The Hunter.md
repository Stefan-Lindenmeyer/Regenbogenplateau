The Hunter
I run from him. 
Every night I have the worst nightmares. Monsters come after me, they tear me apart, they rend my flesh, they torture my patience and grind my mind to dust.
When I think I am safe, hidden even, I turn around and they are there. But I do not fear them, for they are vulnerable. I can rid myself of them. They are just stories. I can give them away, to others, so that they stop terrorizing me. No, them, I do not fear. They are horrifying, but they are nothing compared to him.
The Hunter.
He is a constant, an inevitability, he is real, he is the nightmare. Always in the darkness, he watches. He is ever present and cannot be defeated. No matter what I try, he will not leave my dreams, will not stop hunting me, tormenting me, taking my last shreds of hope and pulverizing them. 
He hunts.
Mind, he is not always pursuing. No, he is smarter, and more cruel even, than the smartest and most cruel of nightmare creatures. He waits. Waits for the perfect time. Waits for when I have dealt with others. When I am exhausted. In my lowest moment, when I am about to be overwhelmed, only then he comes. 
He finds me.
He destroys my enemy, effortlessly, as if to show his utter superiority, and then he smiles at me. His wretched smile, presenting radiant white pointed teeth. His sapphire eyes' stare so intense and cold, I can barely move. He towers over me and raises his sword, ready to strike.
I escape.
I get away just before he hits, every time. He runs after me, calling my name, begging me to stop running, telling me he won't hurt me. And I know he won't. He plays with me like a cat plays with a mouse. He fails to catch up, fails to finish me. But he does not need to, he hunts because he revels in the chase, in seeing his prey getting beaten down by his emissaries, again and again, just so he can be the one to incite true fear in its eyes. It shall never forget his presence and never truly escape him. Always two steps behind it, behind me. And so there is only one option left.
I run from him.
