# Erevan Ladrian - Beloved King, Genius Alchemist, Secret Necromancer

On this months episode of Historical Personalities we will unwind the life and achievements of the infamous last elven King of our nation Erevan Ladrian.
If you have not heard of him directly (I don't blame you, it was two and a half millenia in the past), you have surely heard of his descendants who, despite Erevans later reputation, have risen to high nobility status again and now rule 1/6th of this beautiful country. Their family is truly a resilient and aspiring bunch, having almost been eradicated multiple times and coming back from it stronger everytime, but let's not get ahead of ourselves. 
This article will give you a brief overview of Erevans life and major happenings of his time but as always, if you want to learn more, you can buy the extended version with a lot more dates, details and a discussion of scholars in a book shop of your choice for only 8 Copper pieces.

### Healer of the Nation
Erevan Ladrian, born to Arian and Lovara Ladrian, who tragically died during childbirth, was a compassionate young elf, and, after living with the Kings family due to his fathers death in the first battle of Cliffort, which stood in what is now called the Sea of Ruin in the Mountains westward of Milian, the boy took an interest in potionmaking to focus on healing the injured from the raging abberation wars. Due to his timeless nature as an elf he took his time, but studied the field almost to perfection for a good human lifetime which gave him a perfect baseline to invent new techniques and rethink the craft later in his life.
Closer to the end of his research time he began to put theory into practice and joined the military as a supportive force to be reckoned with. Due to his excellent performance in healing the injured and strengthening the fighting forces he quickly climbed the ranks and found himself leading a new seperate alchemy division where he spread his knowledge of the craft and brought many a fight to a victorious end for the kingdom.
Notable examples include:
- The battle of the severed islands against ruthless cannibals which led him to discover alchemical catalysts
- The 30 year siege of Telendor where he furthered the research on elixirs greatly
- The absorption of the agricultural paradise of Homestead with zero casualties on both sides
- The defense of the great planar gate in Rellheim against an invading Aboleth, which was only possible due to a now unknown alchemical technique Erevan developed but never published
Everthing seemed to be going great, but disaster was not far from Erevan. After making Cliffort, the personal castle of the Ladrian family, the Main Base of Operations for the alchemy division it got attacked again by the same perpetrators that killed Erevans old man. They were out for revenge and eradicated most of his kin, including the current ruler Eldan, during a family gathering. At the last moment Erevan averted disaster and pushed back valiantly against the invaders, though many suspect it was his work in the first place since he was now the heir to the throne and he took it gladly. The only other survivor was his cousin, the son of the King, Boreal. But due to his young age and severe health problems after the attack, he could not ascend to his once destined position. The castle was destroyed in the process and never rebuilt due to a multitude of factors.

### Prosperous Rule
After claiming the cane and crown, Erevan made major changes to the kingdom, most often cited is his introduction of outstanding general healthcare due to the rising popularity and development of the alchemy division whose center of operations was now removed from the destroyed castle and placed in the center of the former capital of Elving (which is better known as the Great Quarantine Zone today, more on that later).

Many women vied for his affection but in the end only one of them could win and it was an Elven lady with the beautiful name of Liannae Vendunst, from the house operating the largest library in the Kingdom. She later births Erevans only child Bayonetta (truly an exotic name).

Erevan also hosted Fertility Festivals to thank the gods and farmers for the prosperity and stability of the kingdom during his rule. Many of the most beautiful young girls were invited every year to present their talents in the castle and receive grants that help their families, communities and stimulate the economy. It is speculated that a multitude of vulgar events happened at these gatherings since it was an invite only party in the castle after which a parade rode through the whole city, but none of the visitors ever had anything bad to say about the ceremony and so the accusations fizzled out.

If you lived at the time, you would regularly hear reports and news of assassination attempts at Erevan, which might seem odd to you, but popularity also breeds envy in some people who think they deserve better. Of course none of these attempts succeeded (except for the one that ended his life), but the tales of their evasion were spun into unbelievable heights, so ridiculous was each new one. A widespread theory surmised, that Erevan had some sort of precognition to know exactly where to place his trusted guards or magical protections.

But by far his biggest achievement is what ended the troublesome invasions from extraplanar forces. After forcing back most enemies from offworld subsequent to their killing of about half the population in a surprise attack, Erevan reportedly gathered a large group of arcanists and mages from all over the world to achieve a single feat from which we still benefit to this day. Together they erected a barrier so massive, it spans the entire world and cuts us off from most other planes of existence. The means by which they achieved this and the energy source sustaining this barrier is still an unsolved mystery but it is undeniable that it exists. This started an unprecedented era of peace only disturbed by the occasional elemental explosion or problems with Fey and Evil Shadow Creatures. That meant we could finally concentrate our efforts to war with ourselves for a change after rebuilding the kingdom, though Erevan would not live to see his work for long.

### Unceremonious End
It seems the perfect king had a serious flaw, handling money.
The kingdoms finances were in shambles after a few decades of rule under Erevan. His generous healthcare system consumed gold by the cart load, his grants on the Fertility Festivals lightened the royal treasures a little bit more everytime and much money seemed to vanish into the aether. Though one should not blame everything on Erevan, the kingdom has lost about half its population recently and other nations struggled with similar problems, but it was undeniable, that his rule could not continue this way. In addition to this, the kings public appearances continued to decline and people rightfully asked for a pillar of support and received an empty courtyard as an answer.
Add to that the upcoming Necromancy Purge that enables the Inquisitioners to perform a ruthless nationwide search of necromantic practicioners and happenings which halted all trade and travel for a short time and you have a perfect time for the downfall of a king.
Right when the Main Inquisition Force launched their investigations in Elving, an unforseen assassin took Erevans life in the chaos of the Purge. A short time after that the Inquisition published that Erevan was researching necromantic potions that he may have wanted to distribute through his healthcare system or utilize in other nefarious ways. His wife and child fled into exile while the other high houses shared the throne and the two only returned hundreds of years later to work their way back up into a position of power.
The seat of Erevans private castle has been flooded, though curiously we could not find any further investigation from any party into this matter.
Lastly, over the next centuries, Elving, the seat of Elven power was plagued by a worsening curse that is still a popular destination for adventurers and aspiring moneymakers. One can only guess what the origin of this curse is, but it stands to reason that it has something to do with Erevans demise. We might only find out when someone gets to the center of the Quarantine Zone which is rumored to be the very place where the fallen King took his last breath.

Thus ended the 2500 year reign of the elves who brought us from the end of the Necromantic wars into a peaceful isolation from the outer planes.
