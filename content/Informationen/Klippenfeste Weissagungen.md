Alt:
A life created, a life destroyed.
His kin by chance, connected in death.
Conscripted to the farthest reaches of influence.
Never meant to see the world again.
The return takes its toll of souls.
Though even in loss, they live on.
To protect the kingdom.
Eventually to claim its prize.
And Rise.

Neu:
When the blue strikes and mixes with the rainbow, one life fades as another begins.
Forged with hammer and steel, the lake will create a new master from its vestiges.
The thoughts of the king will far outlive its body.
