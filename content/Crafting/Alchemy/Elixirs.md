Elixirs are the more powerful evolution of potions. While their strength might not necessarily be higher than other alchemical brews, they generally have a much higher duration, some even permanently changing the physique of the consumant. The downside is, that most creatures can only handle the influence of one elixir at a time without detereorating seriously at a rapid pace.

# Crafting 
To craft an Elixir you must know the basics of potioncraft. Only those with Alchemy skill 1 or higher can craft Elixirs.

# Ingredients
To craft an Elixir, unlike with potions or poisons where it is optional, at least one catalyst is a requirement for every recipe.
The rarity needed for an elixir is listed in the Crafting Table. This rarity denotes the rarity that the catalyst must be.
If a catalyst of the listed rarity is used in the recipe, it will be destroyed. 
If a catalyst of a higher rarity than listed is used, it will not be destroyed.
The catalyst can be of any type that the normal ingredients have if not specifically listed.
~~Furthermore, regular exotic ingredients do not work on elixirs since they offer similar effects already.~~

# Innovation
Since elixirs are a relatively new field, one has to be fairly experienced in potioncraft already to invent new recipes or increase their potency with a Critical Success.
Only those with Alchemy skill 2 or higher will be able to attempt it.
