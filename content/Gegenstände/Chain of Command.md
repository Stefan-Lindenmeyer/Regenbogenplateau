Reward for defeating [[Asodias - The Hunter]]

*Wondrous item(chain), legendary,  requires attunement by a spellcaster*

The user can cast the _Command_ spell at will outside of combat. (If things get out of control, maybe even in combat) The spell uses your Spellcasting modifier.

When targeted with a _Command_ spell, the user can delegate the command to one of its creatures, controlled through _Planar Binding_ and the chain.

When casting _Planar Binding_ you can use the chain as a material component instead of the listed one and upcast the spell to level 9. The creature is then bound to you through a partially invisible chain.
