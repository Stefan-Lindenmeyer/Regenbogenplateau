# Soul Amplifier (attunement)
Once per short rest, as an action, project your soul outward letting you seemingly grow up to two sizes with an ethereal glow in your souls color (Spell Enlarge twice for one minute, no concentration).
At the same time you can choose to shape your soul to your desires (Alter Self).

# Soul Synchronizer (attunement)
You may synchronize your soul with one mechanical device while attuned to the Soul Synchronizer. While synchronized, the statistics and rules of the device follow the rules of _Animate Objects_ if it does not already have its own statistics and rules. When synchronizing you can choose one of the following options
- Soul Imprint (Short rest) - Leave an imprint of your soul on the device. Choose one of your features. The device can now use this feature. If the feature uses resources, it gains 1 of the resource. The feature uses your level and proficiency bonus.
- Partial synchronization (Long rest) - Share part of your soul with the mechanical device. Choose a number of your features up to your proficiency modifier. The device can now use these features. If the feature uses a resource, the device gains its own resource pool equal to half your maximum of that resource (rounded down). The features use your level and proficiency bonus otherwise they use the statistics of the device.
- Full synchronization (24h) - Synchronize fully with the device. When fully synchronized the device can use all of your features and has the same stats as you. This cannot be reverted. You are now considered undead and your soul is bound to the device and able to control it if possible, but still "pilots" your original body. To do this, the synchronized device must be in a 500ft. radius of the body. Your body does not get death saving throws. When your original body dies or gets destroyed, to use a body again, you must find one that is compatible with your soul and inhabit it. When the device gets destroyed you die and your soul gets destroyed. After you synchronized, you do not need to be attuned to the Soul Synchronizer anymore for the soul to still be bound to the device.

# Soul Infuser (attunement)
Choose one Item to infuse it with the energy of your soul. While you are attuned to the Soul Infuser it becomes magical and gains one of these benefits while on your person:
- +2 to hit and double the base damage dice
- +2 to AC and all saving throws
- +2 to Spell save DC & Indestructible
You can change the infused item and effect on a short rest. With the DMs approval you may choose to alter the appearance and size of the object upon infusing it. 

# Soul Channeler (attunement by spellcaster)
You channel your innate energy to mix with your spellcasting. 
You may choose to upcast any spell or similar upcastable effect by one level or increase your smite by one level. 
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier.
When you finish a long rest, you regain all expended uses.
