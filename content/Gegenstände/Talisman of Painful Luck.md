Reward for defeating [[Asodias - The Hunter]] (specifically the Pact of the Talisman Fallen Hunter)

_Wondrous item, rare, requires attunement_

As a bonus action you can invoke the power of the talisman and roll a d4. You take damage equal to the number you rolled and your next attack roll or ability check is increased by the same amount. If the check reaches or exceeds 20 (and you are proficient in the skill) after adding the rolled number, it is considered a critical hit / success.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier per short rest.
