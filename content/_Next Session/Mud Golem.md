**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)

### Special Traits

- **Immutable Form**. The golem is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.
- **Magic Resistance**. The mud golem has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Construct Nature**. A golem doesn’t require air, food, drink, or sleep.
- **Mothers Encouragement.** The mud golem can add the Cha Modifier of their current master to their attack rolls if they are within 30ft. of them.
### Actions

- **Multiattack**. The mud golem makes two slam attacks.
- **Slam**. _Melee Weapon Attack_: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit_: 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.
- **Mud Ball**. _Ranged Weapon Attack_: +2 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. _Hit_: 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage, and the target is [blinded](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/conditions/#Blinded) until the end of its next turn.

### Reactions

- **Protect Mother.** If their current master is within 30 ft. of the mud golem and gets attacked, the golem can use its reaction to distract the attacker, increasing the AC of their master by 3 for the attack.
