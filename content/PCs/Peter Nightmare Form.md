**Challenge** PB +4

#### Special Traits

- **Magic Resistance**. The NF has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Magic Weapons**. The Nightmare Form’s melee weapon attacks are magical.
- **Beast Form.** When tamed, the rules for transformation follow the Druids Wild Shape rules. 

#### Actions

- **Multiattack**. The NF makes four punch attacks or two throw attacks or two punch and one throw attack.
- **Punch**. _Melee Weapon Attack_: +11 to hit, reach 10ft., one target. _Hit_: 12 (1d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage. 
- **Throw**. _Ranged Weapon Attack_: +7 to hit, range 30/60ft., one target. _Hit_: 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage. Throw nearby debris or small creatures. The projectile takes the same amount of damage as the target.
- **Nightmare Domain.** Ryouiku Tenkai ごうもんのむま. Domain 30ft. radius. Immovable, needs concentration, guaranteed hit for NF inside domain, _Become that which they Fear._ has no recharge and affects all in domain, Can't use domain again until long rest. Creatures inside can't escape, creatures outside can go in.

#### Bonus Actions
- **Become that which they Fear. (Recharge 5-6)** You cause each creature within 15 feet of you to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 20, Intimidation). The creatures that fail their saving throws are all frightened by you until the end of your next turn. Can't be used until long rest after Domain has been used.
